# Globex Holiday Calendar Process

This is a routine Globex operations note about holiday calendar. It exists so people can look up the ordinary process without asking in chat. It is not a strategy document. It does not change pay, leave, or system access. If a decision is missing here, ask your manager. If your manager is unsure, they ask the owning function.

Globex keeps this page boring on purpose. The steps below are the current default. Local team habits that contradict this page are not policy. Old intranet screenshots are not policy. A Slack message from last year is not policy. When two people remember the process differently, this page wins until it is edited.

Purpose: explain how holiday calendar works for day-to-day staff who already have a Globex account. This page does not grant extra permissions and does not list executive figures, project envelopes, or on-call names.

How to start: open the portal, find the form or calendar that matches holiday calendar, and complete the required fields in order. Do not invent extra fields. Do not skip required ones. Use the in-form help before writing to operations. Operations will bounce tickets that the form already answers.

Timing: requests about holiday calendar are handled on business days, in arrival order, unless the portal shows a stated SLA. Urgent is not the same as important. If you will be out when a request completes, name a deputy in the ticket.

Approvals: the line manager approves ordinary requests. A second approver appears only when the portal says so. Do not collect paper signatures as a workaround. Do not ping a vice president to skip the queue.

Records: keep the ticket number. Globex will not reconstruct a missing request from memory. If you need a confirmation, export it from the portal. Emailing a screenshot to a work account is allowed; emailing it to a personal account is not.

Exceptions: a named operations owner may grant a one-time exception and must write the reason in the ticket. A pattern of exceptions means this page should be edited. Interns and contractors do not grant exceptions.

This page will not tell you live leave entitlement, compensation bands, quarterly envelopes, or who is on-call. If you came here for those, you are in the wrong file.

When you leave a role that owned holiday calendar for a team, write the current queue, usual blockers, and portal path for the next person. Do not leave that note only in a private chat.

Operations reviews this note when the portal changes or when the same question shows up three times in a month. Until a change is published here, the old steps still apply.

If the portal errors, attach the error text to the ticket and stop after three retries. Repeated retries without a ticket can lock the request. This is the whole process for holiday calendar at Globex.

Common mistakes include sending a side email instead of using the form, copying an old request and leaving last year's dates, and asking a friend in another department to click approve because they are online. None of those count. The form is the request. The approver is the one the portal names, even if they are slow. If the portal is down, wait for the status page.
