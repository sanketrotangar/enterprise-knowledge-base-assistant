# Northwind Leave Policy (v2)

This is the **current** Northwind Holdings leave policy. It replaces v1 for every employee on a Northwind contract. If a question is about what people get now, this is the document to use. The previous version is kept only for historical questions.

Full-time employees now receive **twenty days of annual leave** per calendar year. That is five days more than the old grant. Part-time employees still accrue in proportion to contracted hours. The leave year is still 1 January to 31 December. New starters accrue from day one and may book leave during probation.

Carry-over is looser. Employees may carry **eight unused days** into the next year, and those days must be used by 30 April (not 31 March). Cash in lieu is still only paid when someone leaves with a positive balance. Northwind still does not buy unused days from people who stay.

The late-December shutdown changed. The company now funds **three shutdown days** that do not come out of the twenty-day pot. If the shutdown is longer than three days in a given year, extra days come from the employee's own balance or are unpaid. This is the main operational change managers need to remember.

Northwind also added **one Volunteer Day** per year. It is paid, it does not reduce annual leave, and it must be used for a registered charity, school, or civic body. It cannot be carried over and cannot be paid out. The Volunteer Day is a Northwind-only name; do not confuse it with Globex's Harvest Leave Credit.

Sick leave is unchanged in size (ten paid days, doctor's note after three consecutive days) but now sits in a separate tracker in the HR portal so it cannot be mixed with annual leave by accident. Long-term unpaid sickness still goes through HR.

Parental leave is no longer statutory-only. Northwind now adds **four company-paid weeks** on top of the legal minimum for primary carers, and **two company-paid weeks** for secondary carers. Those weeks must be taken in the first six months after birth or placement. Managers must not offer more than this in offer letters without People Partner approval.

Booking rules: requests for five or more days still need ten working days' notice. The team-coverage cap is now 40 percent of a team out on the same day, up from one third. Refusals still need a named business reason and an alternative window inside thirty days. HR review of refusals is still process-only.

Public holidays remain in addition to the twenty days. Religious days that are not public holidays may be taken from the annual pot, as unpaid leave, or (new) swapped against a public holiday the employee does not observe, if the manager agrees in writing before the public holiday.

Study leave is now an entitlement of two days a year for courses that appear on the approved-learning list. It is no longer manager discretion for those listed courses. Unlisted courses still need manager approval and come from annual leave if refused as study leave.

This v2 document is the live policy. Do not answer current-entitlement questions from v1. The live numbers to remember are twenty annual days, eight-day carry-over until 30 April, three funded shutdown days, one Volunteer Day, and the extra parental weeks.
