# Northwind Intern Onboarding

This guide is for every new intern at Northwind Holdings, in any department. Managers of interns should read it too.

The first week is structured. Monday is IT and accounts: laptop, badge, vault, email, and the HR portal. Tuesday is a company overview in the morning and a team overview in the afternoon. Wednesday is shadowing. Thursday is a small first task with a named buddy. Friday is a 20-minute check-in with the manager and the buddy. Interns do not attend the executive staff meeting in week one, or ever, unless the manager's VP invites them for a specific demo.

The buddy is a named employee one or two levels above intern, not the line manager. The buddy's job is tools, "who do I ask", and the first-week task. The manager's job is goals for the internship and feedback. If a buddy is out, the manager names a substitute on day one, not day four.

Goals: by the end of week two the intern and manager write three outcomes for the internship. They live in the HR portal, not in a private doc. Interns should be able to point at those outcomes in week ten. "Help the team" is not an outcome. "Ship the inventory export behind a feature flag" is.

Hours: the intern working week is 35 hours unless the contract says otherwise. Overtime is not expected and is not a badge of honour. Weekend work needs the manager and HR, not just enthusiasm.

Access: interns get production-read on the systems their team uses, not production-write, unless a VP signs a time-boxed exception. They do not get compensation files, board packs, or customer master data exports. If a tool's default group includes those, IT must carve the intern out before handing over the laptop.

Where the intern sits: with the team, not in a separate intern bay. Remote interns join the team's core hours (10:00–15:00 in the team's time zone) on camera for the first two weeks, then as the team already works.

Manager checklist before day one: desk or remote kit, buddy named, first-week task written, accounts requested five working days ahead, this document sent to the intern. If any of those are missing on Monday morning, the intern's first day slips, it does not "make do."

This is a general document. It does not grant access to HR-only, finance-only, or engineering-only runbooks. Those stay in their own families. Interns in HR still cannot see executive compensation because this onboarding page exists.
