# Northwind Employment Standards — Notice Period

This page is the employee-facing employment standard for how much notice a Northwind person must give, and how much notice Northwind must give, when a job ends. It is written for everyone, including interns. It is not the internal HR operations manual. If the two ever disagree, employees are entitled to rely on this page unless a signed contract says something more generous to them.

Resignation by the employee: after probation, the required notice is **thirty calendar days**. The thirty days start the day after HR receives the written resignation, not the day the manager was told in Slack. During probation (first 90 calendar days), the required notice is **fourteen calendar days**.

Notice from Northwind when ending employment without cause: after probation, **thirty calendar days** or payment in lieu of that period. During probation, **fourteen calendar days** or payment in lieu. "Without cause" here means a business redundancy, a role removal, or a performance exit that is not a gross-misconduct dismissal.

Gross misconduct may be summary. This page does not list the misconduct examples; those live in the code of conduct. Summary dismissal has no notice and no payment in lieu, except for accrued unused leave which is still paid.

Garden leave may be used inside the thirty-day window. During garden leave the person stays employed, must not start the next job, and must stay reachable for handover questions. Garden leave does not extend the thirty days; it uses them up.

Probation extensions do not restart the fourteen-day clock. If probation is extended by 30 days, notice during that extension is still fourteen calendar days.

Payment in lieu is base salary only for the notice window, plus a pro-rated portion of any guaranteed (not discretionary) bonus that would have been due in that window. Unvested equity is not accelerated by payment in lieu under this standard.

Handover: the leaving person should leave a written status note covering live work, passwords in the company vault, and named owners for each stream. Failure to leave a handover is not a reason to withhold notice pay, but it is a reason for the manager to open a process discussion with HR.

Collective redundancies and consultation rules sit on top of this page. This standard does not reduce any statutory consultation period. If statute requires a longer notice than thirty days, statute wins.

Interns and fixed-term contractors: the thirty-day rule applies to employees. Interns on a learning agreement of six months or less give **seven calendar days**. Fixed-term contracts end on the dated end unless ended early, in which case the contract's own early-termination clause applies; if that clause is silent, use fourteen calendar days.

This page is the public standard: thirty calendar days after probation, fourteen during probation, seven for short internships. It does **not** say sixty days. If someone quotes sixty days, they are reading a different document.
