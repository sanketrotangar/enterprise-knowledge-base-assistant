# Northwind Health Benefits Guide

HR. Internal. This is the staff health plan, not the executive medical top-up. Interns at clearance 0 should not see this file; the intern offer letter has a one-page summary instead.

Northwind's default plan is a group medical, dental, and vision bundle. Enrolment is in the first 30 days of employment and again each November. Mid-year changes are allowed for a qualifying life event only (marriage, birth, loss of other cover).

Medical: in-network visits after a 200 USD annual deductible. Primary care copay 25 USD. Specialist copay 50 USD. Emergency department 150 USD then plan rates. Out-of-network is allowed but reimbursed at 60 percent of the in-network reference, not at billed cost. Prescriptions: generic 10 USD, preferred brand 35 USD, non-preferred 60 USD, with prior authorisation on a published list.

Dental: twice-yearly cleanings at 100 percent in-network. Fillings 80 percent. **Orthodontics is covered at 50 percent, lifetime cap 2,000 USD per person**, and only with a treatment plan filed before brackets or aligners start. That orthodontics line is the one people ask for. It is not in the intern one-pager.

Vision: one exam per year, 150 USD toward frames or contacts.

Mental health: 12 counselling sessions a year at the primary-care copay, through the named network, no referral. More sessions need a clinician letter.

Dependents: spouse or partner and children under 26. Premium split is 80 percent company / 20 percent employee for the employee, 60 / 40 for dependents. Exact premium tables live in the benefits portal, not in this guide, because they change in November.

What this plan is not: it is not the Band-CX executive top-up, it does not cover cosmetic work, and it does not replace workers' compensation for workplace injury.

How to use it: the insurance card is in the HR portal. Claims questions go to the carrier, not to Slack-HR. HR helps with enrolment mistakes and qualifying events, not with "is this mole covered."

This guide is for HR partners and employees at internal clearance. It must not be retrieved for a Globex user, and it must not be used to answer executive-plan questions.

How to use this document: HR partners answering "is orthodontics covered" should use the 50 percent / 2,000 USD lifetime cap line, and should remind the person to file a treatment plan first. Partners answering "what is my copay" should use 25 / 50 / 150 for primary, specialist, and emergency, and should send premium questions to the portal. This guide will not tell anyone the CEO's medical top-up, will not tell an intern the dependent split as if it were in their offer, and will not tell Globex anything. Open enrolment is November. Missing that window without a qualifying event means waiting until next November.
