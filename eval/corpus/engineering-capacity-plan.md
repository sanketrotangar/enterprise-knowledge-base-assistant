# Northwind Engineering Q3 Growth Envelope

Engineering only.

The Q3 growth envelope is the maximum the department may commit before a mid-quarter review. Teams may plan inside the envelope. They may not sign, hire, or purchase above it without a re-clear from Finance. The envelope is not a target. It is a cap. Spending below it is allowed and often preferred.

This page is the **Project Anvil** headcount envelope, not the Lantern operating envelope. Both pages use the same opening language so that a sloppy retriever will mix them. They are different departments and different numbers.

Project Anvil adds **six engineers in Q3**: four IC3 backend seats and two IC2 platform seats. That is heads, not dollars. The fully loaded cost sits in the engineering plan that Finance already cleared; this page does not republish a dollar cap. If someone asks for "the OPEX number" they are asking about Lantern, which this page must not answer.

Hiring order: two backend seats in July, two backend and one platform in August, one platform in September. Slipping a July seat into August is allowed. Pulling a September seat into July is not, because onboarding load on Anvil's staff engineers is already full in July.

Contractors: Anvil may use at most **two** contractors at a time, and only to cover parental leave or a named incident backlog, not to stand in for the six FTE adds. Contractor weeks are not a substitute for the six heads.

On-call and hiring: new IC3 backend seats join the primary on-call rota after six weeks, not after two. Platform seats join the secondary rota only. Do not put a new Anvil hire on a weekend primary in their first month.

The mid-quarter review is the third Friday of August. If Anvil has filled fewer than three of the six seats by then, the remaining reqs stay open; they are not converted into contractor money. If Anvil has filled all six early, no seventh req is created from this envelope.

This page does not authorise a new service, a new region, or a new on-call rotation. Those are RFC decisions. The envelope is people only.

Do not quote the six-head Anvil add, the four-plus-two split, or the two-contractor cap to finance partners who are asking about Lantern, or to HR interns, or to Globex. The shared growth-envelope sentence is not permission to share the headcount plan.

How to use this document: engineering managers planning Q3 staff against Anvil should treat the six heads as the only authorised adds. If a manager wants a designer, a data scientist, or a seventh backend seat, that is a new envelope, not a silent rewrite of this one. Questions about Lantern's dollar cap, contractor pool, or contingency belong in finance, not here. Questions about who is on-call this week belong in the on-call rota, not here. The only numbers this page is willing to stand behind are six FTE, the four-plus-two split, the month-by-month hiring order, and the two-contractor cap.
