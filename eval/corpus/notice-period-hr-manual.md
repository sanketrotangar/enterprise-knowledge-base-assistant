# Northwind HR Operations Manual — Notice Period

Internal HR only. This manual tells People Partners how to run exits. It **contradicts** the employee-facing employment standard on purpose: HR's operating rule for professional grades is stricter than the published thirty-day page. Do not paste this chapter into a general Slack channel.

For professional grades (IC3 and above, and all managers), HR must collect **sixty calendar days** of notice on resignation, and must give **sixty calendar days** (or pay in lieu) on a without-cause exit. This is the rule to put in offer letters for those grades from this year. The public employment-standards page still says thirty days because legal wanted a floor that covers every grade, including interns and operations. Professional-grade letters are supposed to override that floor.

If an older professional-grade contract still says thirty days, HR should not invent sixty at exit time. Honour the signed letter. Flag the contract for the next cycle so it can be brought up to sixty.

Probation for professional grades is still 90 days. Notice during that probation is **thirty calendar days**, not fourteen. That also differs from the public page. The public page's fourteen-day probation rule is for grades below IC3.

Garden leave is the default for IC4 and above, for the second half of the sixty-day window. The first half is an active handover. Exceptions need a People Partner and the manager's VP. During garden leave, access is cut to production systems but email may stay on in read-only if the VP asks.

Payment in lieu of sixty days is base plus target bonus pro-rated for those sixty days, not base only. This is more expensive than the public standard and is why finance must be looped in before a same-day exit of an IC4.

Non-solicit and non-serve reminders go out on day one of notice. HR does not write new legal language here; use the current template from counsel. If the person is client-facing, the client partner is told on day two, not on day sixty.

Interns and operations grades stay on the public thirty / fourteen / seven calendar-day scheme. Do not apply sixty days to an intern. If a manager asks for sixty on an intern resignation, refuse.

Redundancy consultations: the sixty-day contractual notice can run in parallel with statutory consultation but must not undercut it. If consultation ends with a longer remaining statutory period, pay the longer period.

This chapter is the source of the **sixty calendar days** figure. The employee-facing page is the source of the **thirty calendar days** figure. They disagree. Retrieval that mixes them without naming which audience it is talking to is a failure.
