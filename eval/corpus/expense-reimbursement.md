# Northwind Expense Reimbursement Policy

Finance. Internal. This is how Northwind people get money back for company spend they put on a personal card or cash. It is not the Lantern OPEX envelope and it is not a purchasing policy for vendors over 10,000 USD.

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 45 days of the spend. Older items need a VP note. Pay runs every Tuesday. A claim submitted by Thursday 17:00 is in the next Tuesday run if it is approved.

Receipts: required for any item of **50 USD or more**. Items under 50 USD still need a line in the claim (date, merchant, business purpose) but may go without an image. This is the under-fifty rule. Do not tell people that "no receipts are ever needed" and do not tell people that "every coffee needs a photo."

Categories: travel, meals with a named external party, team meals (manager pre-approval), cloud/dev tools not already on a company account, office supplies when the office store is closed, and customer gifts under 75 USD. Alcohol at a customer dinner is allowed if it is not the majority of the bill and the attendee list is in the claim. Alcohol at an internal team meal is not reimbursed.

Travel: economy by default. Upgrades are personal. Hotels cap at the city rate in the portal. Rideshares to and from airports and customer sites are fine; commuting to the assigned office is not.

What this policy will not pay: parking tickets, spa, mini-bar, personal streaming, home furniture, "founders' dinner" without a CRO or CFO on the attendee list, and political donations.

Approvals: the line manager approves under 1,000 USD. The finance business partner also approves at 1,000 USD and above. The CFO approves at 5,000 USD and above. Splitting a 1,200 USD dinner into two claims to dodge the extra approver is a policy breach.

Currency: claims in the employee's payroll currency where possible. Foreign receipts are converted at the card's posted rate, or at the portal rate on the spend date if cash.

This file does not publish Project Lantern numbers. If a claim is "for Lantern," it still follows this receipt rule; it does not inherit the 1.80 million envelope as a personal spending limit.

How to use this document: employees at internal clearance in finance, and finance business partners supporting other teams, should treat the under-fifty receipt rule, the 45-day window, the Tuesday pay run, and the 1,000 / 5,000 approval steps as the live process. Managers who tell their team "just expense it, we will sort the category later" are creating a cleanup pile for Tuesday. Lost receipts over 50 USD are a VP-note exception, not a habit. Interns should not be the people holding the company card; if an intern must travel, the manager books. This policy does not answer "what is Lantern's OPEX" and does not answer "can I buy a sofa for my home office."
