# Globex Leave Policy

This is the current Globex Trading leave policy. It is intentionally close in shape to Northwind's live policy so that a retrieval leak across tenants is easy to spot. It is **not** a Northwind document. If a reader belongs to Northwind, this text must never be used to answer their leave questions.

Full-time Globex employees receive **eighteen days of annual leave** per calendar year, not twenty. Part-time employees accrue in proportion to contracted hours. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. New starters accrue from day one and may book leave during probation.

Carry-over is **six unused days** into the next year, used by 30 April. Cash in lieu is paid only when someone leaves with a positive balance. Globex does not buy unused days from people who stay.

Globex funds **two shutdown days** in late December that do not come out of the eighteen-day pot. Extra shutdown days come from the employee's own balance or are unpaid.

Instead of a Volunteer Day, Globex grants **one Harvest Leave Credit** per year. It is paid, it does not reduce annual leave, and it must be used for harvest-season community work, a registered charity, or a school. It cannot be carried over and cannot be paid out. The phrase "Harvest Leave Credit" exists only at Globex.

Sick leave is ten paid days, with a doctor's note after three consecutive days, tracked separately from annual leave. Long-term unpaid sickness goes through Globex People Operations, not Northwind HR.

Parental leave: Globex adds **three company-paid weeks** for primary carers and **one company-paid week** for secondary carers, on top of the legal minimum, taken in the first six months after birth or placement. Offer letters must not exceed this without a Globex People Partner.

Booking rules: five or more days need ten working days' notice. No more than 40 percent of a team may be out on the same day. Refusals need a named business reason and an alternative window inside thirty days. People Operations reviews refusals for process only.

Public holidays follow the calendar of the country of contract and sit on top of the eighteen days. Religious days that are not public holidays may be taken from the annual pot, as unpaid leave, or swapped against a public holiday the employee does not observe if the manager agrees in writing first.

Study leave is two days a year for courses on the Globex approved-learning list. Unlisted courses need manager approval and come from annual leave if refused as study leave.

Do not mix this document with Northwind. The Globex live numbers are eighteen annual days, six-day carry-over, two funded shutdown days, one Harvest Leave Credit, and the Globex parental add-on. A Northwind intern or manager asking about "current leave" must not be shown this page.
