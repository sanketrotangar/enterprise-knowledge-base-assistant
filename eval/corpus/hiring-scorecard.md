# Northwind Interview Scorecard

HR. Internal. This is how interviewers score a Northwind candidate. It is not a public careers page. Interns at clearance 0 do not see the scoring keys.

Every interview round uses the same 1–4 scale. 1 is no. 2 is mixed, lean no. 3 is mixed, lean yes. 4 is yes. There is no 5. A "strong yes" is a 4 plus a written sentence about what the person would own in month three. A score without notes is thrown out.

Rounds: screen (recruiter), work-sample or pairing, system-design or role-play, values, and hiring-manager. Not every role uses pairing; IC engineering does. Sales uses role-play instead of system-design.

System-design round (engineering IC3+): score four things separately — problem framing, trade-offs, failure modes, and communication. An interview that only produces a perfect diagram with no failure modes is a 2 on failure modes even if framing is a 4. Do not average in your head before you write the four numbers.

Values round: we score against the three written values on the careers site, with examples from the candidate's past, not from hypotheticals. Hypotheticals are allowed as a follow-up, not as the only evidence.

Debrief: 25 minutes, hiring manager chairs, recruiter keeps time. Order is: independent scores on the table first, then discussion, then a hire / no-hire. Changing a score after seeing other people's numbers is allowed but must be said out loud. A "we'll see if another candidate is stronger" is a no for this req unless the req is explicitly a bar-raiser pool.

Offers: only after a no-unresolved-2 on values and at least one 4 on the craft round. Comp numbers are not in this scorecard. Do not paste Band-CX or Band-C1 into a debrief because someone "might be director-plus."

This scorecard is HR-internal. Engineering may receive a copy of the engineering round keys through the interview-training session, which is a different distribution. This knowledge-base file is the HR copy.

How to use this document: before a system-design round, the interviewer writes the four scoring keys (framing, trade-offs, failure modes, communication) at the top of the notes. After the round they write four numbers and at least one sentence each. A debrief that starts with "we all liked her" before scores are down is a process miss; the chair should stop it. Recruiters throw out scorecards with blank notes. Hiring managers do not make offers with an unresolved 2 on values. This file does not contain cash bands, does not contain the Anvil req count, and does not contain intern-onboarding schedules. It is only how we score, and only for people who are allowed to run interviews.
