# Northwind Remote Work Policy

Internal. Clearance 1. Interns at clearance 0 get the intern-onboarding note ("join core hours") and not this full policy.

Northwind is hybrid by default for people within 90 minutes of an office, and remote-first for people hired into named remote seats. Hybrid people are in the office three days a week unless their manager publishes a two-day pattern for the whole team. Individual one-day deals are not a policy, they are an exception.

Working from home on an office day because of a delivery, a plumber, or a mild cold is fine if the calendar says so. A quiet pattern of never coming in is a conversation, not a policy loophole.

Working from another country: **20 consecutive calendar days** per rolling year without a tax review. Day 21 needs People Operations and Finance, because payroll and permanent-establishment risk sit with them, not with the manager. A month abroad is over the 20-day cap. Weekend days count. This is the number people ask for.

The 20-day cap is not a work-from-anywhere benefit. It is a short-visit cap. It does not change employment country, healthcare country, or the laptop-export rules.

Core hours: teams pick a four-hour overlap. Default is 10:00–15:00 in the team's base zone if they cannot agree. Interns in their first two weeks join that overlap on camera; after that they follow the team.

Equipment: one company laptop, one monitor stipend every three years for remote seats, no home-office furniture on expenses (see the expense policy). VPN is required on public networks. Customer data does not go to personal drives.

Managers: a team that is "remote some days" still needs a written coverage plan for on-call and for customer meetings. This policy does not override the engineering on-call rota.

This file is internal. It should be visible to engineers and to finance and to HR at clearance 1+. It should not be the intern's first-week reading, and it should not leak to Globex.

How to use this document: the live number for working from another country is 20 consecutive calendar days per rolling year. Day 21 needs People Operations and Finance. A full month abroad is over the cap. Hybrid people default to three office days unless the whole team is on a published two-day pattern. Core hours default to 10:00–15:00 if a team cannot agree a four-hour overlap. This policy does not move an employee's country of employment, does not authorise furniture on expenses, and does not override on-call. Interns in week one follow onboarding, not this full text.
